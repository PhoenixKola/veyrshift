# Visual bible — direction proposal, NOT owner-approved

**Mood:** Premium original sci-fi martial-arts fantasy with a restrained near-black, steel-blue architectural environment and energetic amber / pearl / cyan / orchid action accents. Dramatic but readable; more dimensional cel-shaded game than 16-bit JRPG. A warm, crafted, modern, legible interface and expressive silhouettes.

**B00 update (2026-11-05):** Two original generated style boards are recorded in `b00-style-comparison.md`. Candidate B (crisp inked 2.5D) is the proposed first atlas-feasibility test, with A (hand-painted graphic cel shade) retained as a comparison. Neither direction nor any concept asset is owner-approved; no mass production is authorized by this proposal.

**Perspective:** 2.5D authored chambers / angled-top-down combat and an apparent ground plane. Final projection and character rig method are to be selected in B00 through interactive comparison.

**Shape language:** Fractured circles, braided lines, interrupted borders and gyroscopic structures are recurring Weave Engine motifs. Transformations materialize 'possible self' patterns: articulated plates, orbit rings, counterpath echoes and geometry shifts. Originality requires avoiding borrowed heroic spiky golden hair, Gi uniforms, iconic beam poses and recognizable third-party design motifs.

**Character 01 — Kairo Venn (provisional):** Lean kinetic fighter, asymmetric dark plum hair, short split charcoal jacket, bronze kinetic cuffs, distinct athletic stance and energy-ring elements. The current Canvas proxy in the repo is **NOT** the approved master. Compare silhouette at 96px, 64px, grayscale and motion from actual gameplay angle.

**Character 02 — Lyra Kest (provisional):** Gravity acrobat, segmented teal braids, lunar asymmetric shell, gyroscope tools; not a Kairo recolor. Counterbalance bright teal/pearl effects with visible enemy telegraph colors.

**Form 01 — Ember Drive (design only):** A pulsating ember-orange segmented crown or kinetic cuff lattice rather than generic flame hair. Changed movements, striking geometry and ring compression VFX. A brief time-based hue overlay is NOT sufficient.

**Style gate requirements:** Render at least two coherent original art directions in actual game camera: (A) refined hand-painted cel-shaded 2D rigs with depth layers; (B) crisp stylized high-resolution 2D sprites with selected lighting/VFX layers. Compare dynamic legibility against enemy telegraphs. The owner selects the final direction before mass character production.

**Accessibility:** Avoid rapid full-screen flashes. All important attacks must have color-independent shape and timing tells. Particle budgets and camera-shake toggles; reduced-motion mode to be built during later batches.

**Art production:** Approved front/side/back/isometric master sheet -> idle/move/attack/dash/guard/hit/transform rigs or sprites -> atlas manifests -> gameplay rendering screenshot proof. Each asset must be documented in `asset-provenance.md` and reviewed for accidental resemblance to existing IP.
